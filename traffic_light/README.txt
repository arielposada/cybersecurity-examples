Internet Traffic Light - classroom game for two teams

Open index.html (or upload this whole folder to your site). All files use relative paths.

Logo: save your institution's official logo as  images/tecmilenio_logo.png
      (PNG with a transparent or white background). If the file is missing, the corner stays empty.

Questions: edit js/questions.js (35 questions: 28 traffic light, 7 cybersecurity).
Board rules: edit the numbers at the top of js/game.js.
